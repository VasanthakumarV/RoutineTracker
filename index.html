Small habits. A garden that grows with them.

A habit tracker built on behaviour science. Build the habits you want, break the ones you don't, and see where your time goes. It works offline, keeps your data on your device, and syncs end-to-end encrypted through your own Google Drive. No account, no ads.

Build, break, keep

Start tiny habits anchored to something you already do, ride out urges for the ones you're breaking, and keep track of what you already do well.

A garden of your days

Every habit is a plant that grows from seed to bloom as the habit gets stronger, in a garden that follows the time of day.

Reminders and alarms

A nudge at the time you chose. On Android, an alarm can ring for the things that can't slip, and you can tick a habit off right from the notification.

Where time goes

Log time by area, set aims like "at least 30 minutes outside a day", and look back over a week, a month or a year.

Remember why

When a habit starts slipping, the app shows you the reasons you wrote down when you started.

Private by design

Everything is saved on your device first. Sync is optional and encrypted with your passphrase before it leaves the device.

For Apple silicon and Intel Macs. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. After trying to open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

In your browser Beta

Nothing to install

The same app, running in your browser. Your data stays in this browser's storage on your computer, and syncs with your other devices if you connect Google Drive. Browsers can clear a site's data, for example if you don't visit for a while or clear your browsing data, so connect Google Drive to keep a backup. Turn on the app lock to encrypt what's kept in the browser. Use one tab at a time. Private windows usually can't keep the data. Reminders and alarms need the desktop or Android app.
